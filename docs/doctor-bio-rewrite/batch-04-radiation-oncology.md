Dr. Kushal Narang is Associate Director of Radiation Oncology and Cancer Care at Medanta – The Medicity, Delhi NCR, with more than 20 years of experience. His clinical interests cover brain and spine tumors, head and neck cancers, lymphomas, pediatric cancers and genitourinary cancers of the prostate and bladder, and he also treats breast, lung and esophageal cancers along with malignancies of the stomach, liver, rectum and anal canal.

His technical expertise includes CyberKnife radiosurgery, stereotactic radiosurgery and stereotactic body radiation therapy, alongside intensity-modulated radiation therapy, image-guided radiation therapy, 3D conformal radiotherapy and external beam radiotherapy. He performs both intracavitary and interstitial brachytherapy.

After completing his MBBS, Dr. Narang earned an MD in Radiation Oncology at Tata Memorial Hospital, Mumbai. He later obtained a Professional Diploma in Clinical Research from Catalyst Clinical Services in New Delhi, which is reflected in his research role: he serves as principal investigator and co-investigator for multiple ongoing institutional and multi-institutional research projects.

In 2015 he gave an international paper presentation in Yokohama, Japan, on the use of CyberKnife SRS and SBRT for brain tumors and prostate cancers — the two areas where his stereotactic practice is most concentrated. In 2017 his work took third prize in the best paper session at the fifth National Conference of Young Radiation Oncologists of India, and since 2022 he has been Quality Champion of the Department of Radiation Oncology at Medanta in Gurugram.
