Dr. Ameet Mandot is Director and Clinical Lead for Adult Hepatology and Liver Transplant at Gleneagles Hospital in Mumbai, with more than 24 years of experience. His profile records gastroenterology and hepatobiliary medicine together with surgical gastroenterology for liver transplant.

His practice is built on the liver. He manages liver cirrhosis, chronic liver disease, non-alcoholic fatty liver disease, chronic hepatitis B and C, autoimmune liver diseases, portal hypertension and refractory ascites, and also treats celiac disease and performs ERCP.

He holds an MBBS and an MD, and took the DNB in gastroenterology as a national gold medalist for 2007 to 2008. His fellowship in hepatology and liver transplantation was taken at Hospital Beaujon in Clichy, Paris.

Most of his career has been spent inside the liver transplant programs of Mumbai. He has been liver transplant physician at Apollo Hospitals and at Wockhardt Hospitals, honorary consultant to the liver transplant program at Hinduja Hospital, and gastroenterologist and academic head at Bombay Hospital, before his present post at Gleneagles Hospital in Parel.

Two appointments stand outside the clinical work. He was made academic head of the DNB gastroenterology training program at Bombay Hospital in 2017, and honorary gastroenterologist to the Governor of Maharashtra in 2019.

He has published over 20 papers in indexed medical journals, including contributions to the Indian Journal of Gastroenterology, Gastrointestinal Endoscopy and Endoscopy. Between the Paris fellowship, the transplant program appointments and a training program headship, his record describes a hepatologist whose work sits as much in transplant medicine and teaching as in general liver practice.
