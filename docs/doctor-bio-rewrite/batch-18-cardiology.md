His list covers coronary angiography, coronary angioplasty and stenting, diagnostic cardiac catheterization, echocardiography, pacemaker implantation, TAVI or TAVR and the MitraClip procedure, with coronary artery disease, valvular and rheumatic heart disease, cardiomyopathy, atrial fibrillation and heart failure on the medical side.

His qualifications were taken over fifteen years and on two continents. He read M.B.B.S. at Govt. Medical College in Nagpur in 1977 and took his M.D. in medicine there in 1981. His D.Card. came from the University of London at the Royal Postgraduate Medical School, Hammersmith Hospital, in 1989, and his DNB in cardiology from Nizam's Institute of Medical Sciences in Hyderabad in 1992. His interventional training was taken both at Nizam's Institute of Medical Sciences and at the Charles De Nicole Hospital of the University of Ruien in France. He practises at the KIMS Superspeciality Hospital in Begumpet.

He was President of the Andhra Pradesh Cardiology Society of India between 2007 and 2008, sits on the advisory board of the Krishna Institute of Medical Sciences, and is a life member of the Cardiology Society of India, the Academy of Medical Sciences and the Indian Medical Association.

He has been an investigator in international randomized trials including MICHELANGELO OASIS-6, PLATO, OASIS-7, the XIENCE V Registry and LIFENOX, and has operated live cases at CSI Hyderabad in 2007.
