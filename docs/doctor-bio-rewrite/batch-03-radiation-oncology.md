Her practice also covers the wider use of radiation therapy for cancer across the disease sites managed within the department, which allows treatment to be shaped around the individual tumour and the patient's overall plan of care.

Dr. Garg earned her MBBS at BJ Medical College, Ahmedabad, and completed her MD in Radiation Oncology at the Gujarat Cancer & Research Institute, also in Ahmedabad. She subsequently took advanced training in radiation techniques at Mannheim, Germany. Her career has included affiliations with Max Healthcare, Rajiv Gandhi Cancer and Research Institute and Medanta – The Medicity, giving her experience across several multidisciplinary cancer centres in the region.

Her professional memberships connect her with both Indian and international oncology organisations: the American Society for Therapeutic Oncology, the Indian Society of Oncology, the Association of Radiation Oncologists of India and the Oncology Forum. For patients travelling to Delhi NCR for breast or gynaecological cancer treatment, her profile combines a quarter-century of radiation oncology practice, a defined organ-site focus, and training in conformal, image-guided, stereotactic and brachytherapy techniques.
