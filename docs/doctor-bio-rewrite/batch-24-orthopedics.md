### Proposed BIO

Dr. Sumeet Rastogi is Principal Consultant in Orthopaedics and Joint Replacement at Max Smart Super Speciality Hospital, Saket, in the Delhi NCR, with more than 26 years of experience.

His list is the broadest kind of general orthopaedic practice. Arthroplasty covers total knee and total hip replacement, partial knee replacement, hip resurfacing, shoulder replacement, ankle replacement and revision knee and hip replacement. Sports and arthroscopic work covers ACL and PCL reconstruction, meniscus repair, rotator cuff repair and shoulder instability. He performs carpal tunnel release and bunion surgery for hallux valgus, and treats trigger finger, tennis elbow, golfer's elbow and plantar fasciitis. He also performs limb lengthening, fracture fixation, non-union repair, tendon repair and joint injection.

He read MBBS at JLN Medical College in 1995 and took the DNB in orthopaedic surgery at Sir Ganga Ram Hospital in New Delhi in 1999. His later training was taken abroad: advanced Zimmer trauma at Chulalongkorn University in Bangkok in 2009, an AO basic course in 2012, and a joint replacement fellowship at the Istituto Clinico S Anna Hospital in Brescia in 2015.

He is a DNB certified teacher in orthopaedics and a certified thesis guide for the National Board of Examinations, and has conducted international outpatient clinics in Africa, Iraq and Afghanistan. He has more than 25 publications, is associate editor of a Thieme India book on complex primary knee replacement, and has written chapters on short stem design and on instability following knee replacement.
