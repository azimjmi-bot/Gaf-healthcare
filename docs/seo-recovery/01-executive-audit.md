## Executive Summary

GAF Healthcare operates a large programmatic and curated medical-tourism platform (`13,247` unique URLs across live sitemaps; `15,826` unique reachable catalog, profile, editorial, and country URLs across English and Arabic, excluding pagination/query variants). Despite significant content investment — `94` published English curated treatments, `111` published English blogs (median `3,270` words), `2,602` doctor profiles, `49` JCI/NABH-aligned hospital campuses, `419` treatment cost datasets, and `37` country-to-India travel hubs — organic search visibility in the available 28-day Google Search Console snapshot (`2026-10-20` to `2026-11-16`, `a/Chart.csv`) is **18 clicks and 3,530 impressions** (`4,134` summed across the top-1,000 exported URLs in `Pages-28d.csv`).

This weak organic performance is **not caused by a single catastrophic block** (`robots.txt` allows crawling, Googlebot receives identical full SSR HTML as browsers, and the recent `ar-publication-index` `Symbol.for` optimisation eliminated cold EN→AR R2 hydration CPU spikes). Rather, organic visibility is suppressed by a **compounding interaction of four confirmed failure modes**:

1. **Severe Programmatic Index Bloat & Self-Cannibalisation (`~3,700–4,200` redundant URLs):** English sitemap (`8,294` URLs) exposes `4,296` programmatic cost/doctor/hospital facet combinations (`51.8%` of `sitemap-en.xml`), including **`964` hospital facets and `118` doctor facets whose entity list is 100% identical to their parent URL** (`1,082` exact-parent duplicates), plus `2,090` city-procedure cost sheets and `1,676` non-India country cost combinations without custom copy. While Arabic (`4,843` sitemap URLs) suppresses `3,637` thin/duplicate facets, English leaves them live, self-canonical, and sitemap-listed.
2. **Broken Sitemap / Indexation / Hreflang Signals on Live Production:**
   - **All `147` `/ar/costs/india/<city>/<specialty>` URLs** in `sitemap-ar.xml` emit `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, follow">` AND `hreflang` tags because `src/app/costs/directory.tsx` skips specialty-profile lookup whenever `locale !== "en"`.
   - `withLocaleMetadata()` (`src/lib/i18n/metadata.ts`) emits `hreflang` alternates on `noindex` pages (and directory routes overwrite `robots` afterwards), creating non-reciprocal/contradictory signals across EN/AR.
   - `costs/directory.tsx` overwrites the national canonical on city-procedure cost pages lacking `city.page`, and `hospitals/directory.tsx` leaves `?page=2+` indexable while canonicalizing to page 1.
3. **Broken Internal Equity Architecture & Weak E-E-A-T:**
   - `DoctorProfileGraph` (`src/components/doctor-profile-graph.tsx`) outputs `<h2>Related radiation treatments</h2>` on **`2,490 / 2,602` non-radiation doctors** (verified live on cardiologist `/doctors/dr-ashok-seth`), links **`284` doctors to `noindex` cost URLs**, and provides **zero direct links from doctors to `/treatments/*` pages**.
   - **`61 / 94` (`64.9%`)** curated treatments receive **zero inbound links from blogs**; **`2,348 / 2,602` (`90.2%`)** doctors and **`17 / 49` (`34.7%`)** hospitals are unlinked from any curated treatment.
   - All `111` published blogs rely on generic desk bylines (`GAF Healthcare clinical desk` / `GAF Healthcare Editorial`) with no named physician author or medical reviewer in DOM or `MedicalWebPage` JSON-LD — a critical gap for YMYL medical queries.
4. **Unresolved Legacy Migration & Crawl Waste (`2,691` GSC 404s; `GAF_NEGATIVE_CACHE: OFF`):**
   - In the exported top-1,000 GSC 28-day performing pages (`Pages-28d.csv`), **`359` URLs (`931` impressions, `22.5%` of top-1,000 impressions)** currently return **404** (including `331` unpublished `/ru/*` URLs generating `792` impressions / `19.2%` of top-1,000 impressions, plus `/resources/blog/*`, `/ireland/*`, and nested `/treatments/<spec>/<leaf>`).
   - Across the `1,000`-row GSC 404 export (`Table_8b0f.original.csv`, capped from `~2,691` reported 404s), **`~720` URLs still return 404**. Because `GAF_NEGATIVE_CACHE` is `OFF` on apex, every bot hit to those 404s enters the 8-slot `MAX_INFLIGHT_RENDERS` queue and triggers an uncached Next.js App Router SSR 404 render (`30–45 KB`, up to `5.2–5.5s` on cold isolates).

## Section 15: Direct Answers to the 10 Final Questions

### 1. Why is a website with over 10,000 URLs receiving so little organic visibility?
Because **size (`13,247` sitemap URLs) is acting against the site, not for it**. Roughly **half of the English sitemap (`4,296 / 8,294` URLs)** consists of multi-dimensional programmatic facets (`/costs/...`, `/doctors/...`, `/hospitals/...`) — including **`1,082` exact-parent duplicates**, **`2,090` templated city-cost permutations**, and **`284+` thin/overlapping listings** — while high-margin commercial pages (`/treatments/*` and top-level `/costs/india/<specialty>`) lack internal link concentration, external authority, and named clinical E-E-A-T authorship. Simultaneously, **`~720` legacy URLs in GSC's 404 sample still 404**, **`359` of the top-1,000 impression-bearing URLs in the last 28 days are currently 404**, and **`147` Arabic cost URLs in `sitemap-ar.xml` output `noindex`** while claiming `hreflang` parity. Googlebot is spending crawl and evaluation budget on thousands of near-identical or broken URLs instead of consolidating PageRank onto ~200–350 high-intent commercial landing pages.

### 2. Is the main problem technical SEO, content quality, domain authority, indexing, or a combination?
It is a **combination of all four, dominated by (1) Programmatic Content Differentiation / Index Bloat and (2) Technical Indexing & Migration Signals, compounded by (3) Weak Domain Authority / Missing E-E-A-T**:
- **Technical SEO & Indexing (Immediate P0/P1 blockers):** `147` `sitemap-ar.xml` URLs returning `noindex`; `hreflang` emitted on `noindex` & non-canonical pages; `costs/directory.tsx` canonical overwrite; `/hospitals?page=2+` indexable; `~720` unresolved GSC 404s (`359` actively getting impressions in `Pages-28d.csv`); `DoctorProfileGraph` mislabeling non-radiation doctors and linking `284` doctors to `noindex` pages.
- **Programmatic Content Quality:** `1,082` exact-parent duplicate EN facets, `1,324` facets with `>=90%` overlap, `37` secondary country hubs sharing up to `64%` structural copy, `1,676` unpopulated non-India cost pages, and thin/unverified doctor profiles (`2,477/2,602` missing research, `1,118/2,602` missing education).
