### 2. Direct Answers to Section 4 Questions (3–14)

- **Q3 (Are templates producing excessive duplicate content?):** **Yes.** Across `5,761` `facetCandidatePaths()`, **`964` EN hospital facets and `118` EN doctor facets (`1,082` total)** return the **exact same set of entities as their parent URL**, and **`1,132` hospital + `192` doctor EN facets (`1,324`) share `>=90%` overlap** with their parent (`src/lib/i18n/facet-canonical.ts` explicitly skips English!). Additionally, `2,090` city-procedure cost pages (`5` cities × `418` treatments) vary primarily by city name, local hospital list, and interpolated cost modifiers.
- **Q4 (Are city pages genuinely differentiated?):** **Weakly.** City cost views (`Delhi NCR`, `Mumbai`, `Bengaluru`, `Chennai`, `Hyderabad`) inject city-specific hospital/doctor cards and interpolated USD ranges, while prose structure follows the `cost-articles` city `.page` template (`418/419` treatments have all 5 city `.page` entries; only `breast-conserving-surgery-lumpectomy` lacks `.page`). City-level doctor/hospital facets without curated intro copy (`hospitals/directory.tsx`, `doctors/directory.tsx`) share identical boilerplate headings/FAQs differing only by city filter cards.
- **Q5 (Are treatment pages sufficiently comprehensive?):** **Uneven.** Curated treatments (`94` EN) average `~416–659` words depending on specialty — solid for quick overviews and doctor/hospital linking, but thin compared to competitive medical tourism guides (`1,500–2,500+` words with clinical staging, side-effect timelines, patient eligibility, and named specialist review).
- **Q6 (Are doctor profiles unique and useful?):** **Partially, with serious template/data gaps.** `0 / 2,602` have empty bios (`1,708` are `50–150` words; `894` are `>=150` words). However, **`1,118` (`43.0%`) lack education**, **`2,477` (`95.2%`) lack research/publications**, and **`1,933` (`74.3%`) lack awards**. Worse, **`DoctorProfileGraph` displays `<h2>Related radiation treatments</h2>` on `2,490 / 2,602` non-radiation doctors**.
- **Q7 (Are hospital profiles unique and useful?):** **Moderately useful (`49` campuses).** Profiles list city, accreditations, specialties, doctors, and procedures, though many lack granular international-patient desk workflows, bed/ICU metrics, and verified outcome/volume citations, and `17 / 49` are disconnected from curated treatment pages.
- **Q8 (Are cost pages providing actual useful pricing information?):** **National sheets: Yes (`419` treatments with USD `partnerRange`, itemized cost breakdowns, stay duration). City/Country permutations: Diluted.** All `1,676` non-India country cost combinations (`4` non-primary countries × `419`) have **`0` custom `.page` prose** and render generic templated pricing via `CostsCountryView`.
- **Q9 (Are there pages with very little original content?):** **Yes.** `15` EN doctor facets and `35` EN hospital facets have `<3` matching entities (`99` hospital facets are faculty-only `noindex`), `5` EN blogs are under `500` words (minimum `83` words), `1,676` non-India cost views lack custom prose, and secondary country hubs (`37` total) share heavy boilerplate (e.g., `armenia` vs `belarus` shares `263 / 414` long lines = **`64%`** line overlap, vs `15%` for primary hubs like `kenya` vs `tanzania`).
- **Q10 (Excessive combinations of country, city, specialty, procedure URLs?):** **Yes.** `2,637` EN cost facets + `1,434` EN doctor facets + `1,335` EN hospital facets = **`5,406` facet/cost URLs** in `sitemap-en.xml` alone (`65.2%` of the English sitemap) competing over just `49` hospitals and `2,602` doctors.
- **Q11 (Filters or query parameters generating crawlable duplicate URLs?):** **Yes.** `robots.txt` (`Allow: /`) permits crawling of `?page=`, `?hospital=`, `?experience=`, `?city=`, `?specialty=`. While `/doctors?page=2+` emits `noindex, follow`, **`/hospitals?page=2+` omits `noindex`** and stays indexable with canonical pointing to page 1.
- **Q12–Q13 (Pages with no meaningful search intent or insufficient supporting data?):** **Yes.** Depth-4 hospital facets with 1–2 entities (e.g., `/hospitals/india/pune/bariatric-surgery/...` or exact-parent duplicates), and `284` doctor-linked `/costs/india/<city>/<specialty>` pages that fail the quality threshold (`noindex`) because the city lacks sufficient verified hospital/procedure inventory.
- **Q14 (Internal linking adequate between treatments, doctors, hospitals?):** **No — critically fragmented** (see Section 7 below).

## Section 7: Internal Linking & Site Structure

| Link Pathway | Audited State on `b5cefe53` | Severity / Structural Gap |
|---|---|---|
| **Homepage → Treatments & Hubs** | Links cleanly to `/treatments`, `/specialties`, `/doctors`, `/hospitals`, `/costs`, `/blogs`, and all `37` `ORIGIN_COUNTRY_HUBS`. | High-priority individual `/treatments/*` commercial pages receive limited direct homepage anchor share compared to `37` uniform country hubs. |
| **Treatments → Doctors & Hospitals** | `94` curated treatments link cleanly (`0` broken links) to selected doctors and hospitals (`~8–12` doctors, `~6–14` hospitals each). | **`2,348 / 2,602` (`90.2%`) doctors** and **`17 / 49` (`34.7%`) hospitals** receive **0** links from any curated treatment. |
| **Doctors → Treatments / Costs / Blogs** | `DoctorProfileGraph` links doctors to `/doctors/india/<city>/<spec>`, `/doctors/india/<spec>/<proc>`, `/costs/india/<city>/<spec>`, related costs, and 3 blogs. | **Critical bugs:** **1)** **0 direct links from `2,602` doctors to `/treatments/*` pages**; **2)** `<h2>Related radiation treatments</h2>` hardcoded on **`2,490` non-radiation doctors**; **3)** **`284` doctors link to `noindex` cost URLs**; **4)** Anchor text uses generic `"View cost guide"` / `"<Specialty> treatment in <City>, India"`. |
| **Blogs → Treatments** | All `111` EN blogs have `0` broken or `noindex` `relatedLinks`. | **`61 / 94` (`64.9%`) curated treatments receive `0` inbound links from any blog** (heavy concentration on breast cancer & radiation oncology blogs). |
| **Costs → Treatments** | Cost sheets link to hospitals/doctors and specialty hubs. | **`218 / 419` (`52.0%`) cost treatments have `0` inbound links from any curated treatment** (and vice versa on many non-oncology sheets). |
| **Breadcrumbs & EN↔AR Nav** | JSON-LD `BreadcrumbList` matches URL hierarchy cleanly on blogs, treatments, and profiles. | EN↔AR language switcher & `hreflang` fail reciprocity on the `147` `noindex` AR city-specialty cost pages and `/blogs/breast-cancer-follow-up-tests`. |

**Answers (Section 7 Q1–Q7):** Important hubs are within 2–3 clicks, but deep city-procedure cost pages and >2,300 doctors sit 4+ clicks deep via pagination/filters; high-value treatments in Urology/Neurosurgery/GI/IVF/Orthopaedics are isolated from blogs and doctor profiles; `284` doctors push equity into `noindex` URLs; and `DoctorProfileGraph` anchor/heading text is misleading on 95.7% of doctors. **Recommendation:** Wire bidirectional curated links between `/treatments/*` ↔ `/costs/*` ↔ `/blogs/*` ↔ matching `/doctors/*` & `/hospitals/*`, fix the `DoctorProfileGraph` heading & `noindex` link filter, and surface the Top 100 commercial URLs from specialty hubs and contextual cross-links.

### E-E-A-T, Medical Claims, International Patient Logistics & EN/AR Parity (Q4–Q10)
- **Cannibalisation (Q4):** Moderate-to-high between `/treatments/<x>-in-india` vs `/costs/india/<spec>/<x>` vs `/blogs/<x>-cost-in-india` (e.g., `breast-cancer-treatment-in-india` vs cost sheets vs `breast-cancer-treatment-cost-in-india`), worsened by weak internal hierarchy telling Google which URL is the primary commercial hub vs cost reference vs educational article.
- **Medical Author, Reviewer & Trust Signals (Q7–Q8 — Critical YMYL Weakness):** Inspection of `src/app/blogs/[slug]/page.tsx` (`blogStructuredData`) and all `111` EN blogs confirms **zero named physician authors or medical reviewers**. All `111` blogs display generic desk attribution (`GAF Healthcare clinical desk` / `GAF Healthcare Editorial`), and `medicalWebPageJsonLd` omits `author` / `reviewedBy` person entities (emitting only `lastReviewed` and `Organization` publisher). Do **not** fabricate credentials; any clinical review attribution added later must use real, verifiable partner/board clinicians.
- **International Patient Requirements (Q9):** Well-represented on the `37` `ORIGIN_COUNTRY_HUBS` (visa, currency, travel logistics), but inconsistently integrated into the `94` `/treatments/*` pages and `419` cost sheets beyond generic CTAs.
- **EN vs AR Parity (Q10):** Incomplete (`58/94` curated treatments, `73/111` blogs, `0/37` country hubs), and Arabic commercial city-specialty discovery is currently neutralized by the `147`-URL `noindex` bug in `costs/directory.tsx`.
