## What Is Blepharoplasty?

**Blepharoplasty is surgery performed on the eyelids.** It can involve removing excess skin, selectively removing or repositioning fat, and addressing other tissues that contribute to a heavy, puffy or aged eyelid appearance.

The operation may involve the **upper eyelids, lower eyelids or both**.

Upper blepharoplasty commonly addresses excess or lax upper-eyelid skin. Lower blepharoplasty may address lower-eyelid bags, excess skin and the relationship between eyelid fat and surrounding tissues.

Blepharoplasty is not a procedure in which as much skin or fat as possible is removed.

Modern eyelid surgery generally aims to preserve or restore an appropriate eyelid contour while maintaining eyelid function and protecting the ocular surface. The exact amount of tissue treatment depends on anatomy.

## Why Do People Have Blepharoplasty?

The reasons can be cosmetic, functional or both.

Common concerns include excess skin on the upper eyelids, hooded or heavy-looking upper eyelids, upper-eyelid skin affecting peripheral vision, puffy upper eyelids, bags beneath the eyes, excess lower-eyelid skin, certain forms of lower-eyelid laxity, age-related changes around the eyes, asymmetry between the eyelids, and a tired appearance around the eyes.

Upper blepharoplasty can have functional benefits in appropriately selected patients. Published systematic reviews have reported improvements in visual field and some vision-related quality-of-life outcomes after upper blepharoplasty.

## Types of Blepharoplasty in India

### 1. Upper blepharoplasty

**Upper blepharoplasty** treats the upper eyelid.

The surgeon typically places the incision within the natural upper-eyelid crease. Depending on anatomy, excess skin may be removed and selected fat or muscle may be addressed.

The goal is not simply to create a larger eyelid opening. The surgeon must consider the brow, eyelid crease, skin quality, fat distribution and eyelid function.

Upper eyelid surgery may be performed for cosmetic reasons or because excess skin is contributing to visual-field obstruction.

### 2. Lower blepharoplasty

**Lower blepharoplasty** focuses on the lower eyelid and under-eye region.

It may be used to address prominent fat, lower-eyelid bags and selected cases of excess skin.

Depending on anatomy, the surgeon may use an incision just below the eyelashes or an incision inside the lower eyelid, known as a transconjunctival approach.

Lower eyelid surgery requires particularly careful planning because the lower eyelid helps protect the eye and maintain the relationship between the eyelid and the eyeball.

### 3. Upper and lower blepharoplasty

Some patients have changes in both the upper and lower eyelids.

In such cases, **combined upper and lower blepharoplasty** may be considered.

The advantage of combining procedures is that the surgeon can assess the entire periorbital region as one anatomical unit. Combining procedures may also affect operating time, recovery and the quotation. Combined work remains on the named blepharoplasty sheet when it is still a listed eyelid case; added brow, ptosis or reconstructive work is quoted after review.

### 4. Functional blepharoplasty

Blepharoplasty is not always purely cosmetic.

In some patients, excess upper-eyelid skin can interfere with peripheral vision. When visual function is affected, eyelid surgery may be considered for a functional indication.

Assessment may include visual-field testing and examination of the eyelids and brow.

Whether treatment qualifies for insurance coverage depends on the patient's country, insurer and the medical indication. Cosmetic eyelid surgery is generally treated differently from medically necessary surgery.

### 5. Cosmetic blepharoplasty

**Cosmetic blepharoplasty** is performed primarily to improve the appearance of the eyelids and surrounding eye area.

Typical concerns include hooded upper eyelids, excess upper-eyelid skin, under-eye bags, puffy lower eyelids and age-related eyelid changes.

A consultation is important because not every concern around the eyes is caused by excess eyelid skin.

Brow descent, true eyelid ptosis, tear-trough hollowing and midface changes can create an appearance that may not be corrected adequately by standard blepharoplasty alone.

## Blepharoplasty vs Ptosis Surgery

These procedures are often confused.

**Blepharoplasty** primarily addresses excess eyelid skin and/or other tissues contributing to eyelid contour.

**Ptosis surgery** addresses drooping of the upper eyelid caused by dysfunction or weakness involving the eyelid lifting mechanism. There is no separate GAF ptosis-repair sheet; that work is quoted after records review.

A patient can have both excess skin and true ptosis.

Treating only the excess skin may not adequately address the underlying eyelid position. A careful examination is therefore important before surgery. Mayo Clinic notes that blepharoplasty may sometimes be combined with ptosis surgery when the upper eyelid itself is drooping.

## Who Is a Candidate for Blepharoplasty?

A suitable candidate generally has a clearly defined concern that can reasonably be addressed by eyelid surgery and understands both the benefits and limitations of the procedure.

Factors considered during assessment include general health, eye health, eyelid anatomy, tear-film and dry-eye symptoms, brow position, eyelid position, skin excess, fat distribution, previous eye or eyelid surgery, smoking status, medications, and expectations.

The American Society of Plastic Surgeons describes appropriate candidates as generally healthy individuals without serious conditions that could impair healing, with realistic goals and without serious eye conditions.

## Blepharoplasty Consultation in India

A good consultation should be more than a discussion about removing excess skin.

The surgeon should examine the eyelids and surrounding structures and determine what is actually causing the patient's concern.

The consultation may include medical history, eye history, previous surgeries, medication review, dry-eye assessment, eyelid examination, brow assessment, evaluation of eyelid position, assessment of skin excess, assessment of orbital fat, photographic documentation, discussion of surgical options, discussion of risks, recovery planning and expected outcome.

## How Is Blepharoplasty Performed?

The exact operation differs according to whether the upper eyelid, lower eyelid or both are treated.

### Step 1: Surgical planning

The surgeon marks the eyelids before surgery.

Measurements and markings help determine the incision location and the amount of tissue that may need to be treated.

The objective is to create a natural eyelid contour rather than simply remove the maximum possible amount of tissue.

### Step 2: Anaesthesia

Blepharoplasty may be performed using local anaesthesia, often with sedation, or under general anaesthesia depending on the operation and patient.

The choice depends on the extent of surgery, the patient's health, surgeon preference and hospital protocols.

### Step 3: Upper eyelid incision

For upper blepharoplasty, the incision is generally placed within the natural eyelid crease.

This allows the resulting scar to be concealed within an anatomical fold once healed.

The surgeon then addresses the amount of excess skin and, where appropriate, underlying tissue.

### Step 4: Lower eyelid surgery

For lower blepharoplasty, the incision may be made beneath the eyelashes or through the inside of the lower eyelid.

The approach depends on anatomy and whether the primary concern involves skin, fat or other lower-eyelid changes.

Lower blepharoplasty has evolved considerably, with techniques increasingly emphasizing appropriate fat preservation or repositioning rather than indiscriminate fat removal.

### Step 5: Closure

The incisions are closed using fine sutures or another appropriate closure technique.

Some sutures may need to be removed during follow-up, while others may dissolve or be managed according to the surgical technique.

## How Long Does Blepharoplasty Take?

The duration depends on upper versus lower surgery, one or both eyelids, surgical technique, whether fat repositioning is required, whether ptosis or brow surgery is performed simultaneously, and whether another facial procedure is combined.

A simple upper blepharoplasty is generally less extensive than combined upper-and-lower eyelid surgery.

Your surgeon should provide an individualized estimate after examination.

## Recovery After Blepharoplasty

Recovery is usually manageable, but the eyes can look swollen or bruised during the first several days.

Common early symptoms include swelling, bruising, mild discomfort, tightness, temporary blurred vision, watery eyes, light sensitivity, dry or irritated eyes, and temporary numbness around the eyelids.

These symptoms generally improve progressively.

GAF stay planning is typically **day-care or overnight**. International patients should not plan travel from the shortest published stay.

The American Society of Plastic Surgeons recommends following individualized postoperative instructions, including appropriate wound and eye care, medication use and follow-up.

## What Should You Avoid After Blepharoplasty?

Your surgeon's instructions should take priority, but common postoperative precautions may include avoiding rubbing the eyes, avoiding strenuous exercise during the initial recovery period, avoiding swimming until cleared, avoiding smoking, protecting the eyes from excessive sun and wind, avoiding contact lenses until permitted, taking prescribed medication as instructed, and attending scheduled follow-up appointments.

Mayo Clinic specifically recommends avoiding eye rubbing, strenuous activity and smoking during recovery and advises protecting healing eyelid skin from sun and wind.

## When Can I Return to Work After Blepharoplasty?

The answer depends on your occupation and how quickly swelling and bruising resolve.

People working remotely may return to computer-based work relatively quickly if they feel comfortable.

People whose work involves frequent public interaction may prefer more time because bruising can remain visible for several days.

Physically demanding jobs may require a longer restriction on strenuous activity.

Your surgeon should provide the specific timeline based on your operation.

## Does Blepharoplasty Hurt?

Blepharoplasty is generally associated with discomfort rather than severe pain.

During surgery, anaesthesia is used to prevent pain.

After surgery, patients may experience tightness, soreness, swelling, mild pain or eye irritation.

Severe or rapidly increasing eye pain after surgery should not be considered normal and requires prompt assessment in a **local emergency department**.

## Risks and Complications of Blepharoplasty

Blepharoplasty is commonly performed, but it is still surgery around a highly sensitive anatomical area.

Potential complications include bleeding, infection, swelling, bruising, dry eyes, eye irritation, temporary visual changes, difficulty closing the eyes, eyelid asymmetry, lid lag, ectropion, scarring, changes in skin sensation, need for revision surgery, injury to deeper structures and rare visual complications.

The American Society of Plastic Surgeons specifically lists dry eyes, difficulty closing the eyes, ectropion, infection, bleeding, lid lag, unfavorable scarring and temporary or permanent visual changes among potential risks.

Rare but serious complications affecting vision have been reported. Patients should therefore treat eyelid surgery as real surgery rather than a minor cosmetic treatment.

## Blepharoplasty and Dry Eye

Dry eye deserves particular attention before eyelid surgery.

Some patients experience temporary dryness or irritation after blepharoplasty.

The relationship between surgical technique and ocular-surface symptoms is complex, and patients with pre-existing dry eye should discuss this explicitly with their surgeon.

Recent research continues to evaluate the functional and ocular-surface effects of upper blepharoplasty. A 2025 systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized trials found differences between some surgical techniques in relation to postoperative lagophthalmos, while overall patient satisfaction remained high in the included studies.

This is one reason individualized surgical planning matters.

## Blepharoplasty for Asian and Darker Skin Types

Eyelid anatomy varies substantially between individuals and populations.

Patients of Asian, African, Middle Eastern and South Asian backgrounds may have different eyelid crease patterns, skin characteristics, pigmentation and soft-tissue anatomy.

The surgical plan should therefore be individualized rather than based on a single standardized eyelid shape.

The objective should be an appropriate result for the patient's own facial anatomy and expectations.

## Blepharoplasty for Men

Blepharoplasty is not limited to women.

Men may undergo upper or lower eyelid surgery for both functional and cosmetic reasons.

The surgical design may differ because male and female eyelid and brow characteristics can differ, and surgeons generally consider the patient's natural facial anatomy when planning the procedure.

The goal should be to maintain a natural appearance rather than create an artificially altered eyelid shape.

## Blepharoplasty Before and After

Before-and-after photographs can help patients understand a surgeon's approach.

However, photographs should be interpreted carefully.

Look for patients with similar eyelid anatomy, similar skin quality, similar age range, similar upper or lower eyelid concerns, and similar ethnic and facial characteristics.

A photograph does not guarantee that the same result will be achievable for another patient.

The American Society of Plastic Surgeons notes that results can be long-lasting but that optimal results are not guaranteed in every case and revision surgery may sometimes be necessary.

## How Long Do Blepharoplasty Results Last?

Blepharoplasty results can be long-lasting.

However, surgery does not stop the natural aging process.

The skin, muscles, soft tissues and brow continue to change with age.

Some patients may maintain their improvement for many years, while others may develop additional eyelid or brow changes over time.

Sun protection and good postoperative care can help protect the result.

## Blepharoplasty vs Non-Surgical Alternatives

Not every under-eye or eyelid concern requires surgery.

Depending on the underlying problem, alternatives may include dermal fillers, laser skin resurfacing, chemical peels, energy-based skin treatments, injectable treatments, medical treatment of allergies or inflammation, and lifestyle measures for temporary under-eye puffiness.

## Blepharoplasty vs Brow Lift

Sometimes what looks like excess upper-eyelid skin is partly caused by a low brow.

A brow lift raises the position of the eyebrow, whereas blepharoplasty directly addresses the eyelid.

Some patients may benefit from one procedure, while others may require a combination.

There is no separate GAF brow-lift sheet. Combined brow work is quoted after records review.

The distinction is important because removing too much upper-eyelid skin when brow descent is a major contributor may not produce the desired result.

## Blepharoplasty vs Eye Bag Treatment

“Eye bags” can have several causes.

They may involve prominent orbital fat, skin laxity, tear-trough hollowing, midface changes, fluid retention, allergies or aging-related tissue changes.

The appropriate treatment cannot be determined from the phrase “eye bags” alone.

A specialist assessment helps identify the actual anatomical cause.

## What Results Can You Expect?

The expected result depends on the starting anatomy and surgical plan.

Potential improvements include less excess upper-eyelid skin, a more defined eyelid crease, reduction of selected eyelid bags, a smoother lower eyelid contour, improved visual field in appropriately selected functional cases, and a more rested appearance.

The objective should be improvement while maintaining natural eyelid function.

A surgeon should never promise a particular facial appearance or guarantee perfect symmetry.

## Can Blepharoplasty Improve Vision?

It can in selected patients.

When excess upper-eyelid skin obstructs the superior or peripheral visual field, removing the obstructing tissue can improve the visual field.

Systematic-review evidence has reported functional benefits including increased visual field and improvements in some vision-related quality-of-life measures after upper blepharoplasty.

However, blepharoplasty does not treat every cause of reduced vision.

If the problem is caused by the eye itself, optic nerve disease, refractive error or another condition, eyelid surgery may not improve vision.

## Can Blepharoplasty Correct Dark Circles?

Not necessarily.

Dark circles can result from pigmentation, thin skin, visible blood vessels, tear-trough shadowing, orbital anatomy, skin laxity, or allergies or rubbing.

Blepharoplasty primarily treats structural eyelid changes.

If pigmentation is the primary concern, another treatment approach may be more appropriate.

## Can Blepharoplasty Remove Under-Eye Bags Permanently?

Blepharoplasty can produce long-lasting improvement in appropriately selected patients.

However, aging continues.

The tissues around the eyes will continue to change over time, and new puffiness or laxity can develop.

The American Society of Plastic Surgeons describes eyelid-surgery results as long-lasting while emphasizing that natural aging continues.

## What Happens If the Eyelids Are Asymmetrical?

Some degree of natural facial asymmetry is common.

The two eyes may differ in brow height, eyelid crease, skin excess, fat distribution, eyelid position and orbital anatomy.

Surgery can improve asymmetry in some patients, but perfect symmetry cannot be guaranteed.

The surgeon should identify pre-existing asymmetry before surgery so that expectations are realistic.

## Revision Blepharoplasty

A **revision blepharoplasty** is surgery performed after a previous eyelid operation to address a residual or new problem.

Reasons for revision may include persistent skin excess, asymmetry, scarring, eyelid malposition, contour irregularity, under-correction, over-correction, or changes associated with further aging.

Revision surgery can be more complex because previous surgery changes the normal anatomy.

There is no separate GAF revision-blepharoplasty sheet. These cases are quoted after records review.

## Red Flags After Blepharoplasty

Patients should seek urgent medical assessment in a **local emergency department** if they develop severe or rapidly increasing eye pain, sudden vision loss or major vision changes, significant bleeding, rapidly increasing swelling, difficulty breathing, chest pain, or other symptoms specifically identified by the surgical team.

Mayo Clinic advises patients to seek immediate medical attention for severe new eye pain, bleeding or vision problems after blepharoplasty.

Do not wait for a WhatsApp reply if those symptoms develop.

## Blepharoplasty in India: Frequently Asked Questions

### Is blepharoplasty safe?

Blepharoplasty is an established surgical procedure, but it has potential complications. Safety depends on appropriate patient selection, surgical technique, anaesthesia, facility standards and postoperative monitoring.

### What is the cost of blepharoplasty in India?

Named GAF partner planning is **$1,500–$3,800**, typically day-care or overnight. Actual quotations still vary according to the lids treated, surgeon, hospital, anaesthesia and whether additional procedures are required.

### Is blepharoplasty performed under local or general anaesthesia?

It can be performed under local anaesthesia, often with sedation, or under general anaesthesia depending on the procedure and patient.

### How long does blepharoplasty take?

The duration varies according to whether upper, lower or combined eyelid surgery is performed and whether additional procedures are included.

### How painful is blepharoplasty?

Most patients experience discomfort, tightness and swelling rather than severe pain. Individual experiences vary.

### How long does swelling last after blepharoplasty?

Swelling generally decreases during the first several days and weeks. Bruising and swelling may remain visible for around 10–14 days or longer in some patients, while final healing can take several months.

### When can I return to work?

Some people return to non-strenuous work relatively quickly, while others prefer to wait until visible bruising and swelling have reduced. Your surgeon should provide an individualized recommendation.

### When can I exercise after blepharoplasty?

Strenuous exercise is generally restricted during early healing. Mayo Clinic advises avoiding strenuous activities for about a week unless the surgeon gives different instructions.

### Can blepharoplasty improve peripheral vision?

Yes, in appropriately selected patients whose visual-field obstruction is caused by excess upper-eyelid tissue.

### Can blepharoplasty treat dark circles?

Only if the dark appearance is substantially related to structural factors that the surgery can address. Pigmentation and vascular causes generally require different treatment.

### Can men undergo blepharoplasty?

Yes. Men undergo both upper and lower eyelid surgery for cosmetic and functional reasons.

### Does blepharoplasty leave scars?

Incisions create scars, but upper-eyelid incisions are generally placed within the natural crease. Lower-eyelid scars depend on the surgical approach. Scars usually mature and become less noticeable over time.

### Can blepharoplasty correct droopy eyelids?

It depends on the cause. Excess skin can be treated with blepharoplasty, while true eyelid ptosis may require ptosis surgery, which is quoted after review.

### How long do blepharoplasty results last?

Results can be long-lasting, but natural aging continues and further eyelid or brow changes can occur.

### Can I combine blepharoplasty with another procedure?

In selected patients, blepharoplasty may be combined with procedures such as brow lift, [facelift](/costs/India/Cosmetic-Surgery/Facelift), [rhinoplasty](/treatments/rhinoplasty-in-india) or ptosis surgery. Combinations beyond a listed eyelid case are quoted after review.

### How soon can international patients return home?

There is no universal timetable. International patients should remain in India long enough for appropriate postoperative assessment and should obtain clearance from their treating surgeon before travelling.

### Which city in India is best for blepharoplasty?

There is no single best city. Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai and Hyderabad are live GAF catalog cities. Choose a named plastic, facial plastic or oculoplastic surgeon who already operates the eyelid anatomy you need.
