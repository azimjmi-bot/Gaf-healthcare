## What is heart bypass surgery?

Heart bypass surgery, medically known as **Coronary Artery Bypass Grafting (CABG)**, is an operation used to improve blood flow to the heart.

During CABG, a cardiac surgeon takes a healthy blood vessel from another part of the body, such as the chest or leg, and connects it to the coronary circulation so blood can flow around a narrowed or blocked section of a coronary artery.

The bypass does not remove the underlying coronary artery disease. Instead, it creates an alternative route for blood to reach the heart muscle.

CABG is commonly used for significant coronary artery disease, particularly when there are multiple important blockages or when the anatomy makes bypass surgery more appropriate than another form of revascularisation. Decisions about CABG versus angioplasty and stenting should be made after reviewing the coronary anatomy, symptoms, heart function, other medical conditions and the patient's preferences. The American Heart Association and ACC/AHA/SCAI guidelines emphasise patient-centred decision-making and, when the best revascularisation strategy is uncertain, a multidisciplinary Heart Team approach.

## Why is CABG needed?

The coronary arteries supply oxygen-rich blood to the heart muscle. Over time, cholesterol, inflammatory material and other substances can build up inside these arteries, forming plaques.

When a coronary artery becomes significantly narrowed, the heart muscle may not receive enough oxygen, particularly during physical activity. This can cause **angina**, or chest discomfort.

If a coronary artery becomes critically blocked, it can contribute to a heart attack.

A cardiologist or Heart Team may consider CABG when there is:

- Significant disease involving multiple coronary arteries
- Important left-main coronary artery disease
- Complex coronary anatomy
- Persistent or severe angina despite appropriate medical treatment
- Coronary disease associated with diabetes in selected patients
- Reduced blood supply to important areas of the heart
- Coronary anatomy where CABG is considered more suitable than PCI
- Certain situations following or associated with a heart attack

The decision is individualised. Having a blocked artery does not automatically mean that CABG is required.

## On-pump vs off-pump CABG: does it change the cost?

### On-pump CABG

In conventional CABG, a heart-lung bypass machine may temporarily take over the circulation while the surgeon performs the grafting.

### Off-pump CABG

Off-pump CABG, sometimes called beating-heart surgery, is performed without using the heart-lung machine in the same way.

Neither technique is automatically appropriate for every patient.

The surgical team considers the patient's coronary anatomy, overall health, surgical risk and the surgeon's experience when deciding which technique is appropriate.

The choice of technique can affect hospital resources and therefore the overall cost, but it should be driven primarily by the clinical situation rather than price.

## How is heart bypass surgery performed?

A typical CABG operation takes approximately **3–6 hours**, although the duration can vary.

The NHS describes the main stages as follows:

1. General anaesthesia is administered.
2. The surgeon accesses the heart, traditionally through the breastbone.
3. A healthy blood vessel is harvested, commonly from the chest or leg.
4. The graft is connected so that blood can flow around the blocked portion of the coronary artery.
5. Depending on the surgical approach, a heart-lung bypass machine may be used.
6. The chest is closed after the grafts have been completed.
7. The patient is transferred to intensive care for monitoring.

The exact surgical approach varies according to the patient's anatomy and the surgeon's treatment plan. Step-by-step theatre detail sits on [CABG Surgery in India](/treatments/cabg-surgery-in-india).

## Frequently asked questions about heart bypass surgery cost in India

## What is the average cost of heart bypass surgery in India?

Heart bypass surgery generally costs **$5,500–$14,000** on the live GAF first-time CABG sheet, depending on the hospital, location, surgical complexity, number of grafts, room category and patient's medical condition. US comparison is **$70,000–$200,000**. Neighbouring redo CABG is **$8,500–$20,000**.

## Is $5,500 enough for bypass surgery in India?

It may be sufficient for some patients and hospitals at the lower end of the first-time sheet, but it should not be assumed to be enough for every case. Premium private hospitals, complex CABG, additional investigations, prolonged ICU stays or complications can increase the bill.

## What is the cost of triple bypass surgery in India?

Triple bypass surgery does not have one fixed national price. The cost depends on the hospital, surgeon, grafts, patient's condition, ICU stay and package. A quotation should be obtained after reviewing the coronary angiogram. The live first-time CABG sheet remains **$5,500–$14,000** unless the case is redo, mini or combined valve work.

## What is the cost of quadruple bypass surgery in India?

Quadruple bypass can cost more than a simpler CABG because multiple grafts and more complex coronary disease may require additional operative planning. However, the number of grafts alone does not determine the final hospital bill.

## Is CABG cheaper than bypass surgery?

CABG and heart bypass surgery are the same procedure. CABG stands for **Coronary Artery Bypass Grafting**, while "heart bypass surgery" is the commonly used term.

## How long does bypass surgery take?

CABG commonly takes approximately **3–6 hours**, although the duration depends on the patient's anatomy and the complexity of the operation.

## How many days do I need to stay in an Indian hospital after CABG?

Many patients stay approximately **5–8 days**, although the exact duration depends on recovery. GAF planning is typically **7–14 nights**, then nearby recovery. Cleveland Clinic reports an average stay of five to seven days, while the NHS describes approximately one week for many patients.

## How long does it take to recover from heart bypass surgery?

Many patients require approximately six weeks before returning to a substantial portion of their normal activities, while complete recovery can take around two to three months.

## Is bypass surgery better than angioplasty?

Neither procedure is universally better for every patient. The appropriate treatment depends on coronary anatomy, disease complexity, symptoms, associated conditions, heart function and individual clinical circumstances. Current revascularisation guidance supports patient-centred decision-making and Heart Team assessment when the best approach is uncertain.

## Can international patients undergo CABG in India?

Yes. Many Indian tertiary-care hospitals have international-patient programmes. International patients should send their angiography, echocardiogram, ECG, blood reports and medical history for preliminary evaluation before travelling.

## Does heart bypass surgery cure coronary artery disease?

CABG creates a new route for blood flow around blocked coronary arteries, but it does not eliminate the underlying process that caused coronary artery disease. Long-term medicines, risk-factor control, diet, exercise, smoking cessation and follow-up remain important.

## Does CABG require open-heart surgery?

Traditional CABG is generally performed through an incision in the chest and breastbone. Selected patients may undergo minimally invasive approaches. The NHS notes that keyhole or minimally invasive approaches are possible in some cases but are not suitable for everyone.

## What happens if a patient has diabetes?

Diabetes is an important consideration in coronary disease and may influence the revascularisation strategy and perioperative management. The patient's cardiologist and cardiac surgeon should assess diabetes control, kidney function and overall cardiovascular risk.

## Can a patient with kidney disease undergo CABG?

Patients with kidney disease can undergo CABG, but kidney function affects surgical planning and risk assessment. The cardiac team may modify medications, hydration and monitoring according to the patient's condition.

## How to choose a hospital for heart bypass surgery in India

Price should be only one part of the decision.

Before choosing a hospital, evaluate:

### Cardiac surgery expertise

### Cardiac ICU

## When should you seek urgent medical care?

CABG planning should never delay emergency treatment.

Seek emergency medical care immediately for symptoms such as:

- Severe or persistent chest pain
- Chest pressure or tightness
- Shortness of breath
- Cold sweating
- Fainting
- Sudden weakness
- Severe nausea associated with chest discomfort
- Pain spreading to the arm, shoulder, jaw or back

The World Health Organization describes heart attack as an acute interruption of blood flow to part of the heart muscle and emphasises the need for urgent medical care.
